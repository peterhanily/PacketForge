# Sample captures

Nineteen folders, each with a generated capture, the real Zeek 8.2 logs it produces (`zeek/`), a
README naming what to look for, and an answer key where one exists. The folders hold 26 capture
files, because four samples ship more than one window or more than one vantage, the point in the
network where a sensor sits. Every capture is deterministic: the same inputs produce a
byte-identical file, and it opens in Wireshark with no errors. Regenerate the gallery with
`scripts/make-samples.sh`, which needs `zeek` and `tshark` on the PATH.

## Answer keys

Fourteen samples ship `GROUND_TRUTH.md` and `GROUND_TRUTH.json`. An attack was executed against a
known plan in those, so the labels are the plan. Samples 12, 13 and 14 demonstrate a capability
rather than an attack and ship no answer key. Samples 18 and 19 reconstruct someone else's incident,
so no ground truth exists. They ship a `RECONSTRUCTION` narrative plus a `CLAIMS` layer generated by
`packetforge warrant`, recording which source claim licenses each flow and what is left unmodelled.

## Reconstructions of a real incident

Someone else's incident, built from public sources. Nobody executed it, so no ground truth exists
and the warranting layer stands in for one.

| Sample | What it shows | Packets | Duration | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [18-openai-hf-exploitgym](18-openai-hf-exploitgym/) | The July 2026 agent intrusion, from two disclosure posts carrying no indicators | 4,601 | 10m 19s | 3.2 MB |
| [19-openai-hf-exploitgym-v2](19-openai-hf-exploitgym-v2/) | The same incident from the 2026-07-27 post-mortem, hunt window | 6,863 | 10m 4s | 6.2 MB |

Sample 18 was frozen on 2026-07-23 with every indicator invented from prose, and it fails Gate 4 on
purpose. Sample 19 uses only sources published after that freeze, and it passes. The two are scored
in [`docs/exploitgym-postmortem-delta.md`](../docs/exploitgym-postmortem-delta.md). Sample 19 also
ships `capture.campaign.pcap`: 2,785 packets over 54h 7m and 2.9 MB, the whole attack timeline with
no ambient noise, a quarter of it the agent failing. Both folders carry
`storyline.provenance.pcapng`, the storyline with its warrant attached as per-packet comments.

Everything here is synthetic and inert: fake traffic with true labels, and no real host, credential,
malware sample or document. See [`docs/inert-by-construction.md`](../docs/inert-by-construction.md).
